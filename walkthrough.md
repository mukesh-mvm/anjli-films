# Walkthrough: Anjali Films - Bespoke Cinematography & 6-Step Journey Section

We have created the new section based on the design provided by the user, with **content customized specifically for Anjali Films' luxury wedding cinematography and photography landing page**, fully responsive across all screen sizes.

---

## What Was Added

### 1. Top Split: Cinematography Planned Around You
- **Design Architecture**:
  - **Left Column**: High-resolution editorial cinematography image with smooth rounded corners (`border-radius: 20px`), soft drop shadow, and a frosted glass caption badge (`✦ Master Color Grade & 4K Cinema`).
  - **Right Column**:
    - Pill tag badge: `✦ CINEMATOGRAPHY & PROCESS`.
    - Regal serif heading: `Authentic Wedding Films, Planned Around You`.
    - Lead copy: *"Every wedding film is not a fixed template or formula."*
    - Narrative description: Detailing how Anjali Films crafts bespoke films tailored to each couple's family rituals, venue acoustics, and personal love story.
    - Deliverables prompt: *"Depending on your celebration package, deliverables include:"*
    - **6-Box Deliverables Grid (3 rows x 2 columns)**:
      1. **Cinematic Teaser**: 1-minute high-energy reel for Instagram.
      2. **Full Wedding Film**: 15–30 min story with vows & speech audio.
      3. **Multi-Cam Coverage**: Candid moments & traditional family setups.
      4. **4K Drone Aerials**: Breathtaking venue & baraat vantage points.
      5. **Same-Day Edit (SDE)**: Reception screening teaser for your guests.
      6. **Leatherette Albums**: Handcrafted flush-mount archival print albums.
    - **CTA Button**: `Explore Custom Packages →` (triggers the Web3Forms callback modal).

---

### 2. Bottom Banner: Our 6-Step Cinematic Wedding Journey
- **Design Architecture**:
  - Deep luxury dark-forest container with golden border and drop shadow.
  - Centered golden serif headline: `Our 6-Step Cinematic Wedding Journey`.
  - **6 Golden Circular Step Badges with Connected Timeline**:
    - **Step 1**: `Consultation` — *Dates, rituals & package vision*
    - **Step 2**: `Shot-List Planning` — *Custom timeline & crew assignment*
    - **Step 3**: `Venue Recce & Angles` — *Drone permits & lighting checks*
    - **Step 4**: `Multi-Camera Shoot` — *Senior directors & candid coverage*
    - **Step 5**: `Color Grade & Sound` — *Master editing & licensed music*
    - **Step 6**: `Final Film & Album Delivery` — *4K digital master & print box*

---

### 3. Mobile Responsiveness & Optimization
- **Desktop (1080px+)**: 2-column top layout + 6-step horizontal timeline with continuous dashed gold connector.
- **Tablet (768px – 1080px)**: Clean single-column layout for top split; bottom timeline wraps gracefully into 3 columns x 2 rows without overlapping.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**:
  - Top deliverable boxes adapt smoothly into touch-friendly cards.
  - Bottom timeline renders in a 2-column grid with centered golden badges.
  - CTA button expands to full-width on mobile screens.
